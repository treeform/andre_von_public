chorus.player.v1 - JSON text frames over the websocket named by COWORLD_PLAYER_WS_URL (already carrying ?slot=N&token=T). A Chorus policy is a prompt: the player container's only job is to deliver it; the game server makes every decision by sending that prompt plus the seat's voice, the complete grid, the chord plan, the live piece score, the seat's own counterfactual credit, the other seats' messages and its private notes to Claude once a turn, as one parallel batch for all seats. game->player frames: {"type":"welcome","protocol":"chorus.player.v1","slot":N,"name":str,"voice":str,"base":int,"bars":int,"steps":16,"key":str,"mode":str,"bpm":int} on connect; {"type":"state","slot":N,"name":str,"voice":str,"seat":{voice,voiceName,base,score,onsets,bars:[[16 tokens] per bar],notes},"piece":float,"turn":int,"turns":int,"turnsPlayed":int,"started":bool,"done":bool,"reason":str} after every event - redacted to the seat's OWN voice, its OWN counterfactual credit and the shared piece score (the other seats' credits and notes are not in it; decisions are server-side so this costs the policy nothing); {"type":"final","done":true,"scores":[4 credits],"voices":[4],"names":[4 table aliases],"piece":float,"bars":int,"reason":str,"slot":N} at episode end, after which the player should exit. player->game frames: {"type":"prompt","prompt":str,"scripted":str} - the prompt (max 4000 runes) is the policy, sent right after connecting and again after welcome; scripted "arpeggio" (or "1"/"true"/"yes") plays the built-in arpeggio baseline for that seat, "pedal" the thin pedal baseline, "" means LLM-driven. The reference player reads PLAYER_PROMPT and PLAYER_SCRIPTED from its environment and exits 0 when the socket closes.
