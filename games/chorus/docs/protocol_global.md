Global spectators connect a websocket to /global and receive the full snapshot as JSON after EVERY event: {"type":"state","game":"chorus","seats":[{name,seat,voice,voiceName,base,score,onsets,bars:[[16 tokens] per bar],say,heard:[{seat,say}],notes,pending,lastTarget,lastEdit} x4 by SEAT],"voiceSeat":[4 seat indexes by voice],"grid":[4 voices][bars][16 steps],"chords":[one chord-root degree per bar],"chordNames":[roman numerals],"key":str,"mode":str,"bpm":int,"steps":16,"turn":int,"turns":int,"turnsPlayed":int,"piece":float,"parts":{consonance,leading,rhythm,novelty},"credits":[4 signed floats by seat],"history":[{turn,piece,credits} per resolved turn],"phase":bars|done,"gameDone":bool,"reason":str,"policyNames":[...],"events":[{kind:start|turn|bar|end,...}],"started":bool,"done":bool,"connected":[bool]}. Voices are 0 Bass, 1 Tenor, 2 Alto, 3 Soprano; seats are indexed by slot and mapped to voices through voiceSeat. The events array is the complete append-only transcript: one start, one turn event per opened turn plus one final turn event, four bar events per turn, and one end event. A seat's own frames carry only its own credit, while spectators see all four here and the endcard reveals them. The browser page at /client/global renders the sequencer live; /client/replay plays a recorded episode; the static replay-viewer bundle renders replays hosted by the platform (index.html?replay=<url>).
