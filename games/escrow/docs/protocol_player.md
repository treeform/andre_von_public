escrow.player.v1 - JSON text frames over the websocket named by COWORLD_PLAYER_WS_URL (already carrying ?slot=N&token=T). An Escrow policy is a prompt: the player container's only job is to deliver it; the game server makes every decision by sending that prompt plus the seat's profile, the whole public floor, the escrow board, the recent ledger and its private notes to Claude once a turn, as ONE parallel batch across all four seats. game->player frames: {"type":"welcome","protocol":"escrow.player.v1","slot":N,"name":str,"profile":str,"turns":int} on connect; {"type":"state","slot":N,"name":str,"seat":{profile,score,hearts,stock{ORE,GRAIN,TIMBER,HEARTS},escrowed{...},production,commission,commissionPay,fills},"turn":int,"turns":int,"turnsPlayed":int,"board":[{id,proposer,acceptor,status,lock,ask,due,turnsLeft,cond,then,else,dsl}],"started":bool,"done":bool,"reason":str} after every event - nothing is redacted because nothing is secret except other seats' private notes, which never reach a player socket; {"type":"final","done":true,"slot":N,"scores":[...],"hearts":[...],"fills":[...],"profiles":[...],"names":[aliases],"turns":int,"reason":str} at episode end, after which the player should exit. player->game frames: {"type":"prompt","prompt":str,"scripted":str} - the prompt (max 4000 chars, truncated on rune boundaries) is the policy, sent right after connecting (and again after welcome); scripted "trader" (or "1") plays the built-in trading baseline for that seat, "hoarder" the autarky foil, "" means LLM-driven. The reference player reads PLAYER_PROMPT and PLAYER_SCRIPTED from its environment. The model answers with {"give":[{"to":alias,"n":1-99,"good":ORE|GRAIN|TIMBER|HEARTS}] (max 2), "offer":"<the seven-line contract DSL>" (max 240 chars), "sign":["C3"] (max 2), "say":str (max 160), "notes":str (max 600)}; every field is optional and {} means pass.
