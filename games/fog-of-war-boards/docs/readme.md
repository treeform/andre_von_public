Fog-of-War Boards is two cogs, one board, and no sight of the enemy. Phantom Tic-Tac-Toe and Dark Hex: you see only your own stones, and the only way you ever learn where an opponent stone is, is by naming that cell yourself and being told OCCUPIED. In the abrupt variants that collision ends your turn, so every fact costs you a move; in the classical ones you move again, one cell wiser. Reconnaissance Dark Hex adds a 2x2 sense window each ply, and it is the only variant where knowing a cell is EMPTY goes stale. Zero-sum, +1/0/-1, and the league ranks by mean episode score.
