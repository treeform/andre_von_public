fogboards.player.v1 - JSON text frames over the websocket named by COWORLD_PLAYER_WS_URL (already carrying ?slot=N&token=T). A Fog-of-War Boards policy is just a prompt: reuse the published player runnable with PLAYER_PROMPT, or PLAYER_SCRIPTED=probe|sweep for a baseline. The player container's only job is to deliver its prompt; the game server makes every decision by sending that prompt plus the acting seat's own view (its stones, the opponent stones it has PROVEN, its referee log, its private notes) to Claude. game->player frames: {"type":"welcome","protocol":"fogboards.player.v1","slot":N,"name":"<alias>","seats":2,"mode":"dark-hex","size":5,"abrupt":true,"sense":0,"maxPlies":50} on connect; {"type":"state","slot":N,"name":"<alias>","ply":p,"maxPlies":M,"mode":str,"seat":{score,stones,discovered,probes,distToWin,fallbacks},"toMove":bool,"started":bool,"done":bool,"reason":str,"ending":str} after every event - REDACTED to the seat's own tallies: it carries no board, no cell list, and nothing about the opponent beyond what this seat has proven, and its distToWin is the seat's BELIEVED distance. Decisions are server-side, so this loses a policy nothing. {"type":"final","done":true,"slot":N,"scores":[...],"outcome":[...],"names":[<aliases>],"plies":k,"reason":str,"ending":str} at episode end, after which the player should exit. player->game frames: {"type":"prompt","prompt":str,"scripted":"probe"|"sweep"|true|false} - send on connect and again after welcome (the first send can race slot registration); the latest frame wins; prompts over 4000 characters are truncated on a rune boundary. The server's reply contract to Claude is {"cell":"c4","sense":"b3","guess":["d3","d4"],"say":"...","notes":"..."}: cell must be one of the seat's legal attempts, sense is required only where the variant has reconnaissance, guess is at most 6 cell names and is never a reason to reject a reply, say is 80 characters for the spectators and notes 400 characters handed back to their author only.
