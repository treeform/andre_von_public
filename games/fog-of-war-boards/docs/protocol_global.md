Global spectators connect a websocket to /global and receive the whole snapshot as JSON after every event: {"type":"state","game":"fog-of-war-boards","mode":str,"size":int,"abrupt":bool,"sense":int,"board":["empty"|"seat0"|"seat1", size*size row-major from rank 1] (the TRUTH, spectators only),"seats":[{name, policy, stones, probes, discovered, distToWin, score, known:[cell], sensedEmpty:[{cell,ply}], guess:[cell], say, notes, scripted, fellBack} x2],"mover":int,"ply":int,"maxPlies":int,"plies":int,"lastAttempt":{seat,cell,result},"lastSense":{seat,anchor},"winner":int,"winPath":[cell],"phase":"sensing"|"moving"|"done","gameDone":bool,"reason":str,"ending":str,"policyNames":[...],"events":[{kind:start|sense|attempt|win|end,...}],"started":bool,"connected":[bool,bool]}. Cells are algebraic strings ("c3"), never indices. The events array is append-only and carries the complete transcript. Three browser pages ship with the game: /client/global renders the live table, /client/player renders one seat's redacted view, and /client/replay plays a recorded episode. Hosted replays are the STATIC wasm bundle (index.html?replay=<url>), never a pod.
