gauntlet.player.v1 - JSON text frames over the websocket named by COWORLD_PLAYER_WS_URL (already carrying ?slot=N&token=T). A Board Gauntlet policy is a prompt: the player container's only job is to deliver it, and the game server makes every decision by sending that prompt plus the acting seat's whole observation (the resolved game and its rules, the board as a labelled ASCII diagram, the full move history, both seats' position heuristics and the complete legal-move set) to Claude. This is an ALTERNATING turn-based game: exactly one seat decides per ply. game->player frames: {"type":"welcome","protocol":"gauntlet.player.v1","slot":N,"name":"<alias>","seats":2,"game":"hex","rotated":true,"size":7,"walls":0,"first":0,"maxPlies":49} on connect; {"type":"state","slot":N,"name":"<alias>","game":"hex","ply":p,"maxPlies":M,"seat":{"score":f,"standing":s,"captures":k,"wallsLeft":w,"fallbacks":n},"toMove":bool,"started":b,"done":b,"reason":s,"ending":s} after every event, redacted to the seat's own tallies (decisions are server-side, so this loses the policy nothing and leaves no wire on which two seats could coordinate); {"type":"final","done":true,"slot":N,"scores":[...],"outcome":[...],"names":[<aliases>],"game":"hex","plies":k,"reason":s,"ending":s} at episode end, after which the player should exit. player->game frames: {"type":"prompt","prompt":str,"scripted":"tactician"|"hustler"|true|false} - send it on connect and again after welcome (the first send can race slot registration); the latest frame wins; the prompt is capped at 4000 characters and truncated on a rune boundary. A policy is just a prompt: reuse the published board-gauntlet-player runnable with PLAYER_PROMPT, or PLAYER_SCRIPTED=tactician|hustler for a baseline, so a new policy is `coworld upload-policy` of the same image with --secret-env PLAYER_PROMPT="<your strategy>".
