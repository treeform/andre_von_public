Global spectators connect a websocket to /global and receive the whole snapshot as JSON after every event: {"type":"state","game":"board-gauntlet","board_game":"quoridor","rotated":true,"size":9,"walls":10,"board":["empty","seat0",...] (occupants, row-major from rank 1),"hWalls":[bool...],"vWalls":[bool...],"pawns":[36,76],"wallsLeft":[7,9],"seats":[{name, policy, standing, captures, wallsUsed, wallsLeft, score, say, notes, scripted, fellBack, readout} x2],"mover":0,"ply":14,"maxPlies":80,"plies":14,"legalCount":37,"lastMove":{"seat":0,"move":"e3h","mkind":"wall","capture":""},"eval":0.18,"winner":-1,"winPath":[],"phase":"moving"|"done","gameDone":false,"reason":"","ending":"","policyNames":[...],"events":[{kind:start|move|win|end,...}],"started":true,"connected":[bool,bool]}. `readout` is the one-line per-seat tension string the scorebug draws (TO CONNECT 3, PATH 6 / WALLS 7, PIECES 9 / ROW 4, THREATS 2). Moves and cells are ALGEBRAIC strings ('d', 'c4', 'b2-c3', 'e3h'), never indices. Three browser pages render it: /client/global is the spectator stage, /client/player is the seat's view, and /client/replay plays a recorded episode; hosted replays are served by the STATIC wasm bundle (index.html?replay=<url>), never by a pod.
