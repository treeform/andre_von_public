coguire — Acquire (Sid Sackson), a 4-player stock-market hotel-chain game.

- Board: 108 tiles (cols 1-12 x rows A-I). Each turn: play one tile, buy up to 3 shares, refill.
- 7 corporations in 3 price tiers (Tower/Luxor cheap, American/Worldwide/Festival medium, Imperial/Continental pricey).
- Founding a corporation (placing next to a lone tile) gives the founder 1 free share. Placing between two
  corporations MERGES them: the larger survives; majority/minority shareholders of the defunct one are paid bonuses
  (10x / 5x the share price); each holder then holds, sells, or trades 2-for-1 for surviving shares.
- A corporation of 11+ tiles is SAFE (cannot be absorbed). The game ends when a corporation reaches 41 tiles or every
  corporation is safe; all bonuses pay out, everyone liquidates, and the richest player wins.
- Score = final net worth (cash + held-share value).
