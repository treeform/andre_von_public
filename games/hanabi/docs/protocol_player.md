hanabi.player.v1 - JSON text frames over the websocket named by COWORLD_PLAYER_WS_URL (already carrying ?slot=N&token=T). A Hanabi policy is a PROMPT: the player container's only job is to deliver it; the game server makes every decision by sending that prompt plus the seat's observation to Claude on that seat's turn, one request per turn. game->player frames: {"type":"welcome","protocol":"hanabi.player.v1","slot":N,"name":alias,"seats":4,"handSize":4,"maxTurns":int} on connect; {"type":"state","slot":N,"name":alias,"yourTurn":bool,"turn":int,"maxTurns":int,"observation":str,"score":int,"hintTokens":int,"fuses":int,"deck":int,"started":bool,"done":bool,"reason":str} after every event - REDACTED to that seat: the observation carries the other three hands face-up, this seat's own hand as KNOWLEDGE ONLY (positive hints, negative information, the candidate set, knownPlayable/knownDead, the chop), the fireworks, the discard pile, the tokens, the full public move log and the enumerated LEGAL MOVES, and it never contains this seat's own card identities, the deck, another seat's notes or banner, a policy name or the seed; {"type":"final","done":true,"slot":N,"scores":[4],"score":int,"fireworks":[5],"names":[aliases],"turns":int,"endReason":str,"reason":str} at episode end, after which the player should exit. player->game frames: {"type":"prompt","prompt":str,"scripted":str} - the prompt (max 4000 runes) is the policy, sent right after connecting and again after welcome; scripted "conventions" (or "1") plays the built-in convention-following baseline for that seat, "cautious" the never-misplays baseline, "" means LLM-driven. The reference player reads PLAYER_PROMPT and PLAYER_SCRIPTED from its environment.
