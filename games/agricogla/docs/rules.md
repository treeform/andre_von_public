Agricogla — a worker-placement farming game (Agricola family).

- 14 rounds in 6 stages; harvests after rounds 4, 7, 9, 11, 13, 14.
- Each round, players alternate placing family members one at a time on UNOCCUPIED
  action spaces. A new action space is revealed each round.
- At each harvest: sown fields yield 1 crop each; pay 2 food per family member
  (newborns 1); animals breed (+1 per type with 2+ and room). Food shortfall =
  begging cards at -3 points each.
- Rooms cost 5 of your house material + 2 reed. Renovate wood->clay->stone. Family
  growth needs more rooms than members (except the stage-5 urgent space).
- Fences enclose pastures (1 wood each, 15 lifetime). Pasture capacity 2 animals
  per space, doubled per stable inside; one animal type per pasture (+1 house pet).
- Final score rewards balance across fields, pastures, grain, vegetables, sheep,
  boar, cattle, family size, renovated rooms, and cards; -1 per unused space.
