Gift Refinements is the Trust Game made spatial. Six cogs walk a 24 x 14 foundry floor where eighteen seep pads slowly grow raw tokens. Consuming turns EVERY token you hold into +1 point each, whatever its grade, and empties your hands. Gifting is a beam: it spends one token of your LOWEST held level and hands the cog it hits THREE tokens of the next level up (a maxed token passes through as one). So a pair that bounces one raw token back and forth can turn it into nine and split it - but at every single step the holder can simply cash out and walk behind a pillar. Nobody has to give anything. All the upside requires it. There is no talk channel: the only signals a cog can send are a beam and where it chooses to stand.
