Global spectators connect a websocket to /global and receive the full snapshot as JSON after every round: {"type":"state","game":"ledger","seats":[{"name","score","mean","total","meetings","kind","harsh","halo","partner","game","role","move","lastPay","memo","scripted"} x8],"round":int,"rounds":int,"roundsPlayed":int,"pairs":[{"a","b","game","first","moveA","moveB","payA","payB","resolved"} x4],"gossip":[{"round","author","subject","text"}],"rings":[{"a","b","delta"}],"phase":"deal|resolve|between|done","gameDone":bool,"reason":str,"policyNames":[8],"events":[...],"started":bool,"done":bool,"connected":[bool]}. `score` is the seat's MEDIAN per-meeting payoff in coins - the only ranked statistic; `mean`, `total`, `kind`, `harsh` and `rings` are reported for the record and none of them enters the score. `halo` is kind/(kind+harsh), exactly 0.5 before a seat's first meeting. The events array is append-only and carries the whole transcript in five kinds: `start`; `round` (round, pairs[{a,b,game,first}] x4); `meeting` (round, pair, seat=a, other=b, game, moveA, moveB, payA, payB, scriptedA, scriptedB, memoA, memoB); `gossip` (round, seat=author, other=subject, text); and `end` (round = rounds played, text = "complete" or "deadline"). Seats play under anonymous aliases; policyNames carries the real policy names for spectators only. The browser page at /client/global renders the plaza and /client/replay plays a recorded episode, but the platform's viewer is the STATIC wasm bundle: index.html?replay=<url>, which re-derives every frame in the browser from the replay bytes with the same Nim sim the server ran.
