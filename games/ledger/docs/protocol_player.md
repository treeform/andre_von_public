ledger.player.v1 - JSON text frames over the websocket named by COWORLD_PLAYER_WS_URL (already carrying ?slot=N&token=T). A Ledger policy is a PROMPT: the player container's only job is to deliver it, and the game server makes every decision by sending that prompt plus the seat's own record, this round's meeting with its full numeric rules, the partner's last 8 public meetings, the whole table's medians, this round's four pairings, the public gossip board and the seat's private memo to Claude. game->player frames: {"type":"welcome","protocol":"ledger.player.v1","slot":N,"name":"<alias>","rounds":int} on connect; {"type":"state","slot":N,"name":"<alias>","seat":{"score":float,"mean":float,"total":int,"meetings":int,"kind":int,"harsh":int},"round":int,"rounds":int,"roundsPlayed":int,"started":bool,"done":bool,"reason":str} after every round - REDACTED to the seat's own tallies and the round counter. The redaction is the point: every decision in Ledger is simultaneous, so a seat may not see this round's pairings, any move, or another seat's memo. Decisions are server-side, so nothing is lost - the seat's real view is the observation the server composes for its model call. {"type":"final","done":true,"scores":[8],"mean":[8],"meetings":[8],"names":[8 aliases],"rounds":int,"reason":"complete|deadline"} at episode end, after which the player should exit 0. player->game frames: {"type":"prompt","prompt":str,"scripted":"mirror"|"shark"|""} - prompt at most 4000 characters (truncated server-side on a rune boundary, never mid-character); send it after connect and again after the welcome; the latest frame applies to all later rounds. The published ledger-player runnable reads its prompt from PLAYER_PROMPT and its baseline from PLAYER_SCRIPTED ("mirror" or "shark"; any other non-empty value means mirror, and PLAYER_SCRIPTED wins when both are set), so a new policy is `coworld upload-policy` of the same image with --secret-env PLAYER_PROMPT="<your strategy>".
