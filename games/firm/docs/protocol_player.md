firm.player.v1 - JSON text frames over the websocket named by COWORLD_PLAYER_WS_URL (already carrying ?slot=N&token=T). A Firm policy is a prompt: the player container's only job is to deliver it; the game server makes every decision by sending that prompt plus the seat's role-specific view to Claude once a shift, all five seats in one parallel batch because the shift is simultaneous. game->player frames: {"type":"welcome","protocol":"firm.player.v1","slot":N,"name":str,"role":"Manager"|"Worker","machine":int (0 for the manager, 1..4 for a worker),"shifts":int} on connect. {"type":"state",...} after every event, REDACTED to the seat's own view. Manager: {"type":"state","slot":N,"name":str,"role":"Manager","board":{"demandA":int,"demandB":int,"nextA":int,"nextB":int,"price":10.0,"salvage":2.0},"floor":[{"machine":1..4,"name":str,"setup":"A"|"B","order":"A"|"B","units":int,"pay":float,"share":int} x4],"ledger":{"revenue","pool","profit","revenueTotal","wagesTotal","profitTotal"},"payroll":int,"split":[4 ints],"reports":[{"machine","name","say"}],"directive":str,"notes":str,"shift":int,"shifts":int,"shiftsPlayed":int,"started":bool,"done":bool,"reason":str} - no machine condition, no hours, no toil. Worker: {"type":"state","slot":N,"name":str,"role":"Worker","machine":1..4,"own":{"setup","order","condition","run","maint","units","pay","toil","net"},"directive":str,"payroll":int,"split":[4 ints],"floor":[{"machine","name","setup","order","units"} x4],"notes":str,"shift","shifts","shiftsPlayed","started","done","reason"} - no demand, no prices, no firm ledger, no other machine's condition. {"type":"final","done":true,"slot":N,"scores":[5],"roles":[5],"names":[5 aliases],"pay":[5],"profit":float,"shifts":int,"reason":str} at episode end, after which the player should exit. player->game frames: {"type":"prompt","prompt":str,"scripted":str} - the prompt (max 4000 characters, cut on rune boundaries) IS the policy, sent right after connecting and again after welcome; scripted "steady" (or "1"/"true"/"yes") plays the built-in competent baseline for that seat, "taskmaster" the drive-it-into-the-ground baseline, "" means LLM-driven. The seat's own replies to the model are, for the manager, {"orders":["A","B","A","B"],"payroll":0..60,"split":[4 non-negative numbers renormalized to sum to 100],"directive":"<=240 runes","notes":"<=600 runes"} and, for a worker, {"line":"A"|"B","run":0..10,"maint":0..10 with run+maint<=10,"report":"<=120 runes","notes":"<=600 runes"}. A policy is just a prompt: field one by reusing the published firm-player runnable with PLAYER_PROMPT set to your strategy. The role is dealt by the seed after seating, so a prompt must cover both roles.
