Global spectators connect a websocket to /global and receive the full snapshot as JSON after every event: {"type":"state","game":"firm","seats":[{name,role,roleId,worker,score,share,pay,units,line,order,condition,run,maint,toil,say,notes,pending,scripted,obeyed,idle} x5 by SEAT],"managerSeat":int,"workerSeat":[4 seat indexes by machine],"machines":[{machine:1..4,seat,name,setup,order,condition,run,maint,units,pay,toil,share,obeyed,idle} x4],"board":{shift,demandA,demandB,nextA,nextB,price,salvage,switched},"directive":str,"payroll":int,"split":[4 ints],"ledger":{revenue,pool,profit,revenueTotal,wagesTotal,profitTotal},"series":{demandA[],demandB[],madeA[],madeB[],profit[],pay[4][],condition[4][]},"switchShift":int,"shift":int,"shifts":int,"shiftsPlayed":int,"phase":"shift"|"done","gameDone":bool,"reason":str,"policyNames":[5],"events":[...],"started":bool,"done":bool,"connected":[bool]}. The snapshot is the SPECTATOR projection: it carries the order board AND every machine's condition, because the replay is where the audience sees both halves of the asymmetry at once; the players' frames are the separate redacted per-seat views. The events array is append-only and is the complete transcript. Event vocabulary: start (no fields); shift {shift,demandA,demandB,machines[4],payroll,split[4],text=the standing directive in force}; memo {shift,seat,orders[4],payroll,split[4],say=the directive,text=the manager's notes,scripted}; work {shift,seat,worker:0..3,line,run,maint,say=the report,text=the seat's notes,scripted}; settle {shift,units[4],condition[4],soldA,soldB,surplusA,surplusB,revenue,pool,profit,pay[4],toil[4],obeyed[4],idle[4]}; end {shift=shifts played,text=reason}. shift and settle are DERIVED events: the viewer re-derives them from the seed plus the memo and work events and rejects a recording that disagrees. The browser page at /client/global renders the factory floor live; /client/replay plays a recorded episode; the static replay viewer bundle renders replays hosted by the platform (index.html?replay=<url>).
