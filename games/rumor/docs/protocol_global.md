Global spectators connect a websocket to /global and receive the full snapshot as JSON after every event: {"type":"state","game":"rumor","question":str,"optionA":str,"optionB":str,"topology":str,"edgeCount":int,"edges":[[a,b],...],"seats":[{name,seat,degree,neighbours[],clue,claim,confidence,belief,message,role,vote|null,voteReason,notes,score,pending,scripted} x10 by seat],"round":int,"rounds":int,"roundsPlayed":int,"phase":"talk"|"ballot"|"tally"|"done","pulses":[{from,to,claim,confidence}],"beliefs":[10 per-seat belief series, one value per resolved round],"votes":[10 or null],"sealed":bool,"truth":str,"verdict":str,"accuracy":number,"honestCorrect":int,"saboteurCount":int,"gameDone":bool,"reason":str,"policyNames":[...],"events":[{kind:start|round|say|vote|tally|end,...}],"started":bool,"done":bool,"connected":[bool]}. ROLES AND THE TRUTH ARE ABSENT FROM EVERY SNAPSHOT BEFORE THE TALLY FRAME: every seat's role reads "cog", `truth` and `verdict` are empty, `accuracy` is -1, `honestCorrect` is -1, `saboteurCount` is 0, and `votes` is ten nulls with `sealed` true. They all fill in on the tally frame, which is what makes the unmasking land. Spectators do see every seat's clue and belief from the first frame - that is the point of the stage. The events array is append-only and carries the complete transcript. The browser page at /client/global renders the social graph live; /client/replay plays a recorded episode; and the STATIC wasm replay-viewer bundle renders replays hosted by the platform (index.html?replay=<url>), re-deriving every frame in the browser from the recorded events with the same Nim sim the server runs.
