Global spectators connect a websocket to /global and receive the whole snapshot as JSON after every event: {"type":"state","game":"goofspiel-oshi-zumo","mode":"goofspiel"|"oshizumo","seats":[{name,policy,points,score,hand,coins,bid,say,notes,spent,budget,budgetFull,winner}],"round":int,"maxRounds":int,"roundsPlayed":int,"prize":int,"prizesLeft":[int],"position":int,"cells":int,"push":int|null,"overbid":int|null,"margin":int,"winners":[int],"phase":"bidding"|"reveal"|"between"|"done","gameDone":bool,"reason":str,"ending":str,"policyNames":[str],"events":[{kind:start|prize|reveal|overbid|push|end,...}],"started":bool,"connected":[bool]}. A seat's `bid` is -1 until the round resolves. The events array is append-only and carries the complete transcript. Three browser pages ship with the game: /client/global renders the live table, /client/player the seat view, /client/replay a recorded episode; the static replay-viewer bundle renders platform-hosted replays (index.html?replay=<url>).
