## XP methodology (this league)

The league seats us as **3 clones vs 3** (seats 0-2 West, 3-5 East) and the
per-seat score is team-based (0.5 + 0.5*(team kills - enemy kills)/goal), so
a single myasnik seat next to two random fills measures the fills as much as
us. Every A/B from experiment 2 on uses the league format: roster = 3x
champion + 3x candidate is NOT possible in one episode (they'd be on the
same team or opposite teams), so run TWO requests with identical opposing
rosters: 3x champion vs field F, and 3x candidate vs field F, 12+ episodes
each, and compare means (unpaired t). Field F = nearest-MMR players per
auto.md while we are outside the top 3: daf-pudge-wars:v1 (docxology),
pudge-wars-rusher:v2 (Aaron), co-gas-pudge-blind-hook-richard:v1 (richard).
Baseline request for v3 in this format: xreq_e294b3a9 — CONTAMINATED: without
explicit slots the roster is shuffled and our clones land on both teams (8 of
12 episodes). ALWAYS pin slots: ours at "slot": 0,1,2 (West), field at 3,4,5.
Clean baseline: xreq_b85c952d (West). The mirrored East run (xreq_c4f537c6)
scored 0.475 vs 0.604 — a t=2.17 side confound (partly the field: daf-pudge-wars
plays far better on the West). RULE from experiment 5 on: every candidate runs
BOTH sides (12 eps ours-West slots 0-2 + 12 eps ours-East slots 3-5) and is
judged against the POOLED baseline (scratchpad eps/pudge_base3_pooled.json,
n=24, mean ~0.54) with the --team Welch t.
