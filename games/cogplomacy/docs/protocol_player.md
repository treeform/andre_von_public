cogplomacy.player.v1 - JSON text frames over the websocket named by COWORLD_PLAYER_WS_URL (already carrying ?slot=N&token=T). A Cogplomacy policy is a prompt: the player container's only job is to deliver it; the game server makes every decision by sending that prompt plus the seat's power, the whole board, the ownership table, two years of order history, the press addressed to that seat and its private notes to Claude once per phase, in one parallel batch with every other live seat. game->player frames: {"type":"welcome","protocol":"cogplomacy.player.v1","slot":N,"power":"FRANCE","years":int,"press":bool} on connect; {"type":"state","slot":N,"power":str,"year":int,"season":spring|fall|winter,"phase":press|orders|retreats|builds|done,"years":int,"yearsPlayed":int,"centres":int,"units":[own units],"board":[every unit on the map],"owners":[34 centres and their owner],"counts":[7 centre counts],"inbox":[{from,text,public}],"eliminated":bool,"started":bool,"done":bool,"reason":str} after every event - Diplomacy hides no positions, so a seat sees the whole board; what it never sees is another power's private letters, another power's notes, or anybody's pending orders. {"type":"final","done":true,"scores":[7],"centres":[7],"units":[7],"powers":[7],"names":[7 cog aliases],"years":int,"reason":str,"soloist":str} at episode end, after which the player should exit. player->game frames: {"type":"prompt","prompt":str,"scripted":str} - the prompt (max 4000 runes) IS the policy, sent right after connecting and again after welcome; scripted "expander" (or "1") registers the seat as the built-in greedy baseline, "hedgehog" as the wall, "" means LLM-driven. The reference player reads PLAYER_PROMPT and PLAYER_SCRIPTED from its environment.
