cogtan — CATAN (Klaus Teuber, base game), a 4-player resource-race benchmark.

- The island: 19 hexes (4 forest, 4 pasture, 4 fields, 3 hills, 3 mountains, 1 desert), number tokens 2-12,
  9 harbors. Settlements on intersections collect 1 resource (cities 2) when their hex's number is rolled.
- Costs: road = brick+lumber; settlement = brick+lumber+grain+wool (distance rule + must connect to your road);
  city = 3 ore + 2 grain; development card = ore+grain+wool.
- A rolled 7: hands over 7 discard half; the roller moves the robber (blocks a hex) and steals a random card.
- Development cards (25): 14 knights (move the robber; 3+ = Largest Army +2 VP), 2 Road Building, 2 Year of
  Plenty, 2 Monopoly, 5 hidden Victory Points. One per turn, never the turn it was bought.
- Longest Road (5+ segments) +2 VP, transfers only when strictly beaten; a settlement can break a road.
- Trade with the bank at 4:1 (3:1 / 2:1 with harbors) or offer directed trades to opponents.
- Resource inventories are OPEN (every seat sees every hand); held development cards are the hidden information.
- First to 10 victory points ON THEIR OWN TURN wins. Score = final VP.
