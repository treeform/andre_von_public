  (playerstats PER_SEAT=1): a seat's score is the mean of min(comb, prof)
  over ALL its lives, the unfinished last life counted at its level when
  the episode ends (7 of 8 seats match exactly; one off by 1/6). So every

  per 90 episodes (networks 314-319).

  both searches for them (v52 peers, v57 stall search) raised deaths.

high-level enemies: min at life end 7.13 vs 6.65, comb at tick 300 5.4)
while we lose ~0.3 (kills per life 14.9 vs 16.3, min at life end 6.08 vs
6.44). Our XP wins therefore overstate our round standing. Candidate fix
to the METHOD (needs the user's ok, the rule says top 3): evaluate with a
roster that mirrors the round field (us + the five other round players).
Round replays: experiments/rounds/<number>/.
- Rounds vs XP, level rows: in rounds the networks fight above-level
  enemies 40-43% (we 32%), level gap -0.3 to -0.6 (we -1.4) and end lives
