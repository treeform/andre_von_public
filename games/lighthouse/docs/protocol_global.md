Global spectators connect a websocket to /global and receive the full board snapshot as JSON after every tick: {"type":"state","game":"lighthouse","seats":[{name,role,status,pos,keys,lastMove,blocked,window,notes,messages,scripted,pending} x4],"grid":[height strings of width chars],"exit":[x,y],"gateOpen":bool,"keysOnFloor":[[x,y]],"keysCollected":int,"keyCount":int,"tick":int,"maxTicks":int,"clock":int,"tideRows":int,"waterLine":int,"message":str,"messageAge":int,"messageCost":int,"escaped":int,"drowned":int,"score":float,"phase":"running"|"done","gameDone":bool,"reason":str,"policyNames":[...],"events":[{kind:start|tick|say|key|escape|drown|end,...}],"started":bool,"done":bool,"connected":[bool]}. Seat 0 is the keeper and has pos null; seats 1-3 are the runners and carry their own 3x3 window. The events array is append-only and carries the complete transcript, including every seat's notes; the viewer re-derives every frame from those events plus config. The browser page at /client/global renders the stage live; the static replay viewer bundle renders replays hosted by the platform as index.html?replay=<url>.
