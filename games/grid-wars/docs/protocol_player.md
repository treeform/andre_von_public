gridwars.player.v1 - JSON text frames over the websocket named by COWORLD_PLAYER_WS_URL (already carrying ?slot=N&token=T). A Grid Wars policy is a prompt: the player container's only job is to deliver it; the game server writes the warrior by sending that prompt plus the seat's alias, its own current script with line numbers, its own diagnostics, the series table and the previous round's ASCII board to Claude once a round, as one parallel batch across all four seats. game->player frames: {"type":"welcome","protocol":"gridwars.player.v1","slot":N,"name":alias,"id":1..4,"rounds":R,"ticks":T} on connect; {"type":"state","slot":N,"name":alias,"seat":{id,color,score,roundsWon,tiles,notes,script,lastRound},"tiles":[4],"aliveLastRound":[4],"round":int,"rounds":int,"roundsPlayed":int,"ticks":int,"done":bool,"reason":str} after every event - REDACTED to this seat: its own warrior, its own script and its own notes plus the public tile counts, never another seat's source, which is sealed before, during and after the round; {"type":"final","done":true,"scores":[4],"tiles":[4],"roundsWon":[4],"names":[4 aliases],"rounds":int,"reason":"complete|deadline"} at episode end, after which the player should exit. player->game frames: {"type":"prompt","prompt":str,"scripted":str} - the prompt (max 4000 characters) is the policy, sent right after connecting and again after welcome; scripted "painter" (or "1") seats the built-in spiral painter, "bomber" the aggressive baseline, "sentry" the always-legal fallback, and "" means LLM-driven. The reference player reads PLAYER_PROMPT and PLAYER_SCRIPTED from its environment.
