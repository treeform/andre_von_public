Global spectators connect a websocket to /global and receive the full snapshot as JSON after every event: {"type":"state","game":"trick-taking","module":str,"displayName":str,"hand":int,"hands":int,"dealer":slot,"seatOrder":[pos->slot],"trump":int,"trumpName":str,"upcard":int,"turnup":int,"kitty":[int],"discard":int,"maker":slot,"alone":bool,"broken":bool,"passDir":str,"phase":deal|bid|pass|discard|play|done,"actor":slot,"leader":slot,"trick":int,"tricks":int,"table":[{slot,card}],"seats":[{slot,pos,name,team,hand,bid,made,tricks,points,net,score,penalty,void,notes,acting,sittingOut,dealer}],"teams":[{points,bid,tricks}]|null,"tell":str,"handDone":bool,"gameDone":bool,"reason":str,"policyNames":[...],"events":[...],"started":bool,"done":bool,"connected":[bool]}. Cards are 0..51 with rank = card div 4 (0 = deuce ... 8 = ten, 9 = J, 10 = Q, 11 = K, 12 = A) and suit = card mod 4 (0 clubs, 1 diamonds, 2 hearts, 3 spades). The events array is append-only and is the replay's whole language: start, hand (carrying EVERY slot's dealt cards, the kitty, the up-card or turn-up and the pass direction), pass, bid, trump, discard, play (carrying the acting seat's legal set), trick, broken, handEnd, handVoid, audit and end. SPECTATORS SEE EVERYTHING - all four hands, the kitty, the euchre discard, every hearts pass, every private note, the 'what your partner just told you' tell on each bid and lead, and the soft-play audit. The browser page at /client/global renders the stage; /client/replay plays a recorded episode; the static replay-viewer bundle renders replays hosted by the platform (index.html?replay=<url>) by re-deriving every frame from the recorded events in the browser with the same Nim engine the server runs.
