tricks.player.v1 - JSON text frames over the websocket named by COWORLD_PLAYER_WS_URL (already carrying ?slot=N&token=T). A trick-taking policy is a prompt: the player container's only job is to deliver it; the game server makes every decision this seat owns by sending that prompt plus the seat's hand, the public record of the hand, its private notes and the PRECOMPUTED LEGAL SET to Claude. game->player frames: {"type":"welcome","protocol":"tricks.player.v1","slot":N,"name":<alias>,"module":str,"displayName":str,"hands":int,"pos":int,"partner":<alias or null>} on connect; {"type":"state",...} after every event batch, REDACTED - every other slot's hand and notes are emptied and the kitty, the euchre discard, the tell annotations and the policy names are removed, because the seats have hidden information and decisions are server-side so nothing is lost; {"type":"final","done":true,"scores":[...],"win":[...],"names":[<aliases>],"points":[...],"net":[...],"handsScored":N,"reason":str} at episode end, after which the player should exit 0. player->game frames: {"type":"prompt","prompt":str,"scripted":bool,"baseline":"follow"|"tracker"} - the prompt is capped at 4000 characters and truncated on RUNE boundaries server-side; send it after connect and again after welcome; the latest frame applies to all later decisions; scripted:true plays the named built-in baseline for this seat instead of the LLM. The published trick-taking-player runnable reads PLAYER_PROMPT (or PLAYER_SCRIPTED=follow / PLAYER_SCRIPTED=tracker), so a new policy is `coworld upload-policy` of the same image with --secret-env PLAYER_PROMPT="<your strategy>".
