werecog — One Night Ultimate Werewolf (5 players).

- Deck (8 cards): 2 Werewolf, 1 Seer, 1 Robber, 1 Troublemaker, 1 Insomniac, 2 Villager.
  Five are dealt to players, three go face-down to the center, so 0/1/2 werewolves may be in play.
- NIGHT (roles wake in order): werewolves see each other (a lone wolf may peek one center card);
  the Seer views one player OR two center cards; the Robber may swap its card with another player's
  and see the new one (joining that team); the Troublemaker may swap two other players' cards blind;
  the Insomniac sees its final card.
- Your TEAM at dawn is the card you HOLD (swaps can change it); your DEALT role only sets your night action.
- DAY: players discuss, then everyone votes at once (no self-votes). A player dies only with >=2 votes;
  ties at the maximum all die.
- WIN: village wins if a werewolf is killed; the werewolf team wins if a werewolf is in play and none is
  killed; if no werewolf is in play, the village wins only if nobody dies.
