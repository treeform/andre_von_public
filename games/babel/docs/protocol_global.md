Global spectators connect a websocket to /global and receive the full snapshot as JSON after every event: {"type":"state","game":"babel","seats":[{name,score,correct,asSpeaker,asListener,role,partner,notes}],"round":int,"rounds":int,"roundsPlayed":int,"glyphs":[16 canonical glyphs],"perm":[4][16],"pairs":[{speaker,listener,target,lineup[4],tokens|null,pick|null,correct|null}],"phase":speak0|pick0|speak1|pick1|between|done,"gameDone":bool,"reason":str,"policyNames":[...],"events":[{kind:start|round|speak|pick|end,...}],"started":bool,"done":bool,"connected":[bool]}. Scene ids are shape*16+colour*4+count (0..63). The events array is append-only and carries the complete transcript, including every seat's notes. The browser page at /client/global renders the stage; /client/replay plays a recorded episode; the static replay viewer bundle renders replays hosted by the platform (index.html?replay=<url>).
