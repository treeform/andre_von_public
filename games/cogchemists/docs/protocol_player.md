cogchemists.player.v1 - JSON text frames over the websocket named by COWORLD_PLAYER_WS_URL (already carrying ?slot=N&token=T). A Cogchemists policy is a prompt: the player container's only job is to deliver it; the game server makes every decision by sending that prompt plus the seat's hand, the board, the public record, its private experiments and its exact deduction grid to Claude once per phase, as ONE parallel batch for all four seats. game->player frames: {"type":"welcome","protocol":"cogchemists.player.v1","slot":N,"name":str,"rounds":int} on connect; {"type":"state","slot":N,"name":str,"you":{coin,reputation,score,hand[names],mortar,press,grid[8][candidate signature indexes],chemistries,facts[],notes},"table":[4 public seat summaries: seat,name,coin,reputation,score,handCount,mortar,press,published[]],"seals":[{ingredient,claim,claimText,author,authorName,round,endorsers[],status,vindications,burnedBy,roundBurned}],"publicFacts":[{kind:mixFull|mixSign|notSig,...}],"round":int,"rounds":int,"phase":"lab"|"market","demand":str,"legal":[exact legal action strings],"heard":[4 remarks],"started":bool,"done":bool,"reason":str} after every event - REDACTED to exactly what that seat may see: no rival's hand contents, private results, notes, grid or chemistries-left ever crosses this socket, and the chemistry never does; {"type":"final","done":true,"scores":[...],"reputation":[...],"coin":[...],"names":[4 aliases],"rounds":int,"reason":str} at episode end, after which the player should exit. player->game frames: {"type":"prompt","prompt":str,"scripted":str} - the prompt (max 4000 runes, truncated on a rune boundary) is the policy, sent right after connecting and again after welcome; scripted "assayer" (or "1"/true) plays the built-in competent scientist for that seat, "quack" the reckless careerist, "" means LLM-driven. The server's reply schema, one object per decision: {"action":str (<=24 runes; forage, test_student, test_self, transmute, pass in LAB; sell, publish, endorse, debunk, buy, pass in MARKET), "a":str (<=24 runes; a full ingredient name, a unique prefix of >=3 letters, or the index 0-7), "b":str (<=24 runes; the second card, or the reagent for debunk), "signature":str (<=12 runes; publish only - R+G-B+, +-+ and + - + all parse), "artifact":str (<=12 runes; buy only - mortar or press), "say":str (<=140 runes, public seminar remark, dropped when talk is false), "notes":str (<=600 runes, private, fed back verbatim next phase)}. Every cap truncates on RUNE boundaries. A reply that will not parse is retried once and then replaced by the scripted assayer move. A policy is just a prompt: field one by reusing the published cogchemists-player runnable with PLAYER_PROMPT set to your strategy.
