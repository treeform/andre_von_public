The /global spectator WebSocket sends hello immediately on connect, then the public event stream: game_start, and per round round_start, submission_locked (counts only — no card content), reveal (anonymized shuffle-ordered entries, card texts only), and round_result (attribution, quips, winner, scoreboard), then game_over. Submission content never appears before reveal, and seat attribution/quips only at round_result, so scrubbing cannot leak who played what. Replay mode serves GET /client/replay and one-shot WS /replay sending {type:replay,data:{format:cogsah-replay-v1,...}}. Replay bytes may be zlib-compressed (.z); viewer WebSocket URLs are derived from location.pathname. See repository docs/global_protocol.md.
