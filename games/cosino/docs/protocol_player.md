cosino.player.v1 - JSON text frames over the websocket named by COWORLD_PLAYER_WS_URL (already carrying ?slot=N&token=T). An Cosino policy is a prompt: the player container's only job is to deliver it; the game server makes every decision by sending that prompt plus the seat's private cards and the public table state to Claude. game->player frames: {"type":"welcome","protocol":"cosino.player.v1","slot":N,"name":alias,"variant":"kuhn"|"leduc"|"holdem","startingStack":int,"ante":int,"smallBlind":int,"bigBlind":int,"hands":int} on connect; {"type":"state","slot":N,"seats":[{name,stack,bet,net,cards,revealed,folded,allIn,acting,handsWon}],"board":[ints],"pot":int,"hand":int,"pair":int,"mirror":bool,"button":int,"currentBet":int,"events":[...],"done":bool} after every event batch (hole cards other than your own are redacted until a showdown reveals them, and the spectator-only calib/audit events are stripped; cards are ints 0-51, rank=card div 4 with 0=deuce..12=ace, suit=card mod 4 as clubs/diamonds/hearts/spades); {"type":"final","done":true,"scores":[...],"win":[...],"names":[aliases],"net":[...],"handsPlayed":N,"reason":"complete"|"deadline"|"budget"} at the end, after which the player exits 0. player->game frames: {"type":"prompt","prompt":str,"scripted":bool,"baseline":"house"|"rock"} - prompt capped at 4000 characters, truncated on RUNE boundaries server-side; send it on connect and again after welcome (the re-send covers a race with slot registration); the latest frame wins for all later decisions. The published cosino-player runnable reads PLAYER_PROMPT (or PLAYER_SCRIPTED=house|rock for a baseline), so a new policy is `coworld upload-policy` of this same image with --secret-env PLAYER_PROMPT="<your strategy>".
