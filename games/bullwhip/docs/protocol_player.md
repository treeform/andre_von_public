bullwhip.player.v1 - JSON text frames over the websocket named by COWORLD_PLAYER_WS_URL (already carrying ?slot=N&token=T). A Bullwhip policy is a prompt: the player container's only job is to deliver it; the game server makes every decision by sending that prompt plus the seat's role, history table, this week's numbers, neighbours' messages and private notes to Claude once a week. game->player frames: {"type":"welcome","protocol":"bullwhip.player.v1","slot":N,"name":str,"role":str,"weeks":int} on connect; {"type":"state","slot":N,"name":str,"seat":{role,score,inventory,backlog,received,incoming,shipped,shipPipe,costWeek,costTotal,lastOrder},"week":int,"weeks":int,"weeksPlayed":int,"started":bool,"done":bool,"reason":str} after every event - redacted to the seat's own stage because the chain has hidden information (other stages' numbers and the demand script are not for the seats; decisions are server-side so nothing is lost); {"type":"final","done":true,"scores":[...],"costs":[...],"roles":[...],"names":[...],"weeks":int,"reason":str} at episode end, after which the player should exit. player->game frames: {"type":"prompt","prompt":str,"scripted":str} - the prompt (max 4000 chars) is the policy, sent right after connecting (and again after welcome); scripted "basestock" (or "1") plays the built-in base-stock baseline for that seat, "mirror" the pass-through baseline, "" means LLM-driven. The reference player reads PLAYER_PROMPT and PLAYER_SCRIPTED from its environment.
