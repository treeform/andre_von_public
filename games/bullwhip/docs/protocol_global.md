Global spectators connect a websocket to /global and receive the full snapshot as JSON after every event: {"type":"state","game":"bullwhip","seats":[{name,stage,role,score,cost,inventory,backlog,received,incoming,shipped,shipPipe[2],costWeek,costTotal,lastOrder,order|null,say,heard[{stage,say}],notes,pending} x4 by seat],"stageSeat":[4 seat indexes by stage],"week":int,"weeks":int,"weeksPlayed":int,"demand":[customer demand revealed through the current week],"orders":[4 per-stage series of orders placed],"phase":orders|done,"gameDone":bool,"reason":str,"policyNames":[...],"events":[{kind:start|week|order|end,...}],"started":bool,"done":bool,"connected":[bool]}. Stages are 0 Retailer, 1 Wholesaler, 2 Distributor, 3 Factory. The events array is append-only and carries the complete transcript, including every seat's messages and notes and every week's resolved stage states. The browser page at /client/global renders the conveyor and the seismograph; /client/replay plays a recorded episode; the static replay viewer bundle renders replays hosted by the platform (index.html?replay=<url>).
